abbey's story

when i was 17, my best friend who i consider my little brother told me that the song "i am not alone" reminded him of me. this showed me that i was doing pretty okay as a found older sibling, and meant a lot to me. he also sent me "honeybee", since he called me bee as a nickname. a few months after that, he passed away suddenly. i had just discovered both "soliton" and "turn back the clock". these four songs got me out of the numb shock that grief puts you in; the spg improv bits online were what made me laugh in those first few terrible months. i credit spg with helping me through that awful period. thank you spg for everything you gave me in that time- most especially reminding me that my loved ones are always there if i trip or if i fall or if i'm just too scared. thank you also for being an incredible theatrical act which inspires my work in theatrical technology. thanks for upgrade, who helped me learn how to be loud and joyous even when it feels scary. thanks for zero, who helps me remember to wear my heart on my sleeve. thanks for the walter workers for being the theatre tech rep we need in the world!!! thanks for all the things.
