Welcome, internaut!

This SPG Fan Archive is meant to host easily accessible SPG content, run by fans for fans. While right now it just lives on Abbey's Github, we hope to one day host a full, filterable archive of all of SPG's public work, making it easier to find and enjoy! We want to preserve not only the artistry of Steam Powered Giraffe, but also adhere to the band's dedication to creativity and joy by sharing things like fanart and fan-run projects.

Do you have something you'd like archived or shared? Drop me a line at abbjectterror@gmail.com.

Why did you make this?

Fandom Wiki is a wonderful resource, but it's not conducive to things like fanart or website customization. I believe that Steam Powered Giraffe, an independent art project with a whole beautiful fictional world, deserves more than a corporate website that throws ads at you every three seconds. I want a place to organize and understand the world of the band and to give other fans that space as well!
in conclusion: i hope you have fun looking at my mockups. i had a heckuva lot of fun making this and working on it!
